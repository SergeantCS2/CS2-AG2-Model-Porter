# CS2's skeleton - see PORTING-OTHER-GAMES.md. tools/blender_remap_weights.py
# reads this file. CS2 names are case-sensitive: hand_L, not hand_l.
#
# Renaming bones is NOT enough on its own: the mesh must also be posed to match
# CS2's rest pose before it is bound to CS2's skeleton. The guide covers it.
#
# CS2 has four spine bones and ValveBiped usually has four too - Spine, Spine1,
# Spine2 and Spine4 (there is normally no Spine3). The finger metacarpals
# (finger_*_meta_L/R) have no ValveBiped equivalent and are optional.
